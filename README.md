## 📖 About the Project

**প্রগতির পাঠশালা (Pragatir Pathshala)** is a modern educational platform designed to make learning more accessible, organized, and effective.

Created for **Master Sourav Bhattacharjee**, this platform brings multiple educational boards, engineering studies, and government job preparation resources together in one place.

The goal is to provide students with a professional digital learning environment where they can access study materials, practice questions, prepare for examinations, and track their academic progress.

## 🎓 Academic Programs

The platform covers five major educational categories:

| No. | Academic Category | Coverage |
|:---:|---|---|
| 01 | West Bengal Board | Classes 7–10 |
| 02 | CBSE Board | Classes 7–10 |
| 03 | ICSE Board | Classes 7–10 |
| 04 | Diploma in Engineering | Branch & Semester Based |
| 05 | Government Jobs Preparation | Competitive Examinations |

## ✨ Key Features

- 📚 **Multiple Boards:** Learning resources for WB Board, CBSE and ICSE.
- 📝 **Study Materials:** Organized notes and educational resources.
- 📄 **PDF Resources:** Access educational documents and notes.
- 🎯 **Online & Offline Learning:** Designed with offline learning capabilities.
- 🧠 **Practice & Examinations:** Quizzes, MCQs and examination preparation.
- 📊 **Student Progress:** Academic performance and learning progress tracking.
- 👨‍🏫 **Teacher Dashboard:** Educational content and course management.
- 📢 **Announcements:** Important academic updates.
- 🎨 **Modern UI:** Clean, responsive and student-friendly interface.
